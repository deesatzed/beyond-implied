# GOAL — report

## Purpose

One honest writeup a stranger can read in 15 minutes and verify in one command. It reports the
pre-registered result whatever its sign, and its failures as plainly as its wins.

## Inputs

- `report/data_test.md` (Phase 2).
- Metric tables and figures (Phases 4–7).
- The ledger and the `prereg-v1` tag.

## Outputs

- `report/data_test.md` — events per season, quality failures by reason, season averages vs ORATS.
- `report/results.md` — the writeup.
- `report/figures/` — three figures:
  1. Risk-at-coverage curves: scaffold vs best baseline (held-out seasons).
  2. Implied vs realized move by season (shows regime rotation).
  3. Hypothetical straddle P&L distribution with expected shortfall marked.

## Writeup outline

1. The question, in one paragraph (incumbent forecast; encompassing).
2. Data and event definitions; link to the pre-registration tag.
3. Baselines and the reproduced ratio effect.
4. Primary result (H1) with clustered errors and cluster count.
5. Risk at coverage (H2) and what the gates abstained on.
6. Third-order calibration and P&L shape.
7. Control arena (H3).
8. What failed, limitations, and what would change the conclusion.
9. How to verify: `make test`, `bi ledger verify`.

## Build steps

1. Phase 2: write `data_test.md` and decide go/stop.
2. Phase 8: draft `results.md` from the outline; figures generated by script, not by hand.
3. Phase 8: update the top-level README with the headline result and the verification command.
4. Phase 8: post; then send directly to named contacts with one line on why it matters to them.

## Done when

- [ ] `results.md` reports H1 exactly as pre-registered.
- [ ] Figures regenerate from a single command.
- [ ] A cold reader can clone, test, verify, and read in 15 minutes.

## Pitfalls

- Burying a null result. Lead with it if that is what happened.
- Claiming trading value. This is a forecasting study; P&L is a decision-level diagnostic only.
